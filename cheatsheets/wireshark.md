# Wireshark

- Packet List: one row per packet
- Packet Details: the packet broken into layers
- Packet Bytes: raw hex and ASCII
- Follow stream: right-click a packet, Follow, TCP/HTTP Stream
- Export a file: File, Export Objects
- Verify a file: `md5sum <file>` on Linux, `Get-FileHash <file> -Algorithm MD5` in PowerShell
